# Shramify – Informal Worker Management Platform

Shramify is a web application designed to connect informal workers such as electricians, plumbers, maids, and laborers directly with customers. The platform helps customers find reliable workers easily while supporting fair wages and reducing the role of middlemen.

## Features

- Worker registration and profile management
- Customer and worker interaction
- Worker verification
- Search for different types of workers
- Ratings and reviews
- Easy access to local services
- Simple and user-friendly interface

## Problem Statement

Informal workers often depend on middlemen to find customers, which can reduce their earnings and limit their opportunities. Customers also face difficulty in finding trusted and verified workers.

Shramify provides a digital platform that connects workers directly with customers, improving accessibility, trust, and employment opportunities.

## How It Works

1. Worker registers on the platform.
2. Worker creates a profile with their service details.
3. Customers can search for required services.
4. Customers can view available worker information.
5. Customers can choose suitable workers based on their details and ratings.
6. Ratings and reviews help build trust between workers and customers.
